# The ApexPlanet Data Story: From Wrangling to Revenue

## 1. The Objective
Our objective was to transform raw, inconsistent data into actionable business intelligence, identifying the key drivers of growth for ApexPlanet across customer segments and product categories.

## 2. The Analysis Journey

### Part 1: Establishing the Foundation
We began with a raw dataset riddled with inconsistencies—duplicate transactions, missing customer names, and negative order amounts. 
- **The Result**: A robust data cleaning pipeline that ensures data integrity (e.g., median imputation for missing values, standardizing DOB/Purchase formats).

### Part 2: Understanding the Market
Exploratory analysis of 1000 transactions revealed a clear leader in our inventory.
- **Key Insight**: Electronics is our "Star" category, generating over **$104,000** in revenue.
- **Demographics**: While spending is balanced across ages, the **35-44 age group** emerged as the highest average spenders.

### Part 3: Loyalty and Retention
A longitudinal cohort study showed that our customers aren't just one-time buyers.
- **Retention**: We maintain a stable **30-50% retention rate** after the third month.
- **Growth**: Recent cohorts (e.g., July 2025) show improved "Day-1" retention (63.49%), signaling better onboarding and initial customer satisfaction.

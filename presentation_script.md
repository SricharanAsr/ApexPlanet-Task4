# Stakeholder Presentation Script: 7-10 Minutes

## Intro (0:00 - 1:00)
"Good day everyone. Today I'm going to take you through the story behind ApexPlanet's data—how we moved from raw numbers to validated strategies that will drive our next phase of growth."
"In this presentation, we'll cover our data foundation, the key demographics driving our revenue, and the statistical proof that supports our recommendations."

## The Foundation: Data Wrangling (1:00 - 2:30)
"We started with Task 1: Data Immersion. We inherited what most real-world data looks like—messy. We built an automated pipeline to handle duplicates and missing values."
"Why does this matter? Because a business strategy is only as good as the data it sits on. By standardizing our dates and handling anomalies, we ensured that every KPI we discuss today is accurate and reliable."

## The Market Landscape (2:30 - 4:00)
"In our Exploratory phase, we found that Electronics isn't just a category; it's our engine. It generates nearly double the revenue of the next closest category, Home Goods."
"We also noticed a trend: our customers in the 35 to 44 age range seemed to be spending more per order. But we didn't just guess—we dug deeper."

## The Deep Dive: Retention & Cohorts (4:00 - 5:30)
"Task 3 looked at longevity. We found that after the initial purchase, about 30-50% of our customers become 'loyalists' by month three."
"The most exciting part? Our July 2025 cohort showed a significant jump in retention compared to earlier months. This tells us our platform is getting stickier as we evolve."

## Statistical Validation (5:30 - 7:30)
"To add rigor, we performed a hypothesis test. We compared the 35-44 segment to the 25-34 segment."
"The results were clear: a P-value of 0.03 means the $100+ advantage in AOV for the older group is statistically significant. This isn't just a coincidence; it's a targetable opportunity."

## Conclusion & Call to Action (7:30 - 10:00)
"So, where do we go from here?"
"First, we capitalize on the 'Muscle'. We need targeted campaigns for high-ticket Electronics focused on the 35-44 demographic."
"Second, we replicate the July success. We need to identify what worked for that cohort and bake it into our standard onboarding."
"And finally, we bridge the gap for our younger users with tiered options that grow with their spending power."
"Thank you. Any questions?"
